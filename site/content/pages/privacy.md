**Last Updated: April 2026**

LiquorNewsUSA ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website liquornewsusa.com. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our site.

## Information We Collect

**Analytics Data** - We use Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymous usage data including pages visited, time on site, device type, and general geographic location. This data does not personally identify you.

**Newsletter Subscriptions** - When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. This is processed by our email service provider, Beehiiv, under their privacy policy.

**Cookies** - We use essential cookies to ensure the website functions properly. Third-party cookies may be set by our analytics and advertising partners (Google Analytics, Google AdSense).

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to operate and improve the website, deliver our newsletter, serve relevant advertising, and analyze site traffic and content performance.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your email address with anyone outside of our email service provider.

## Third-Party Services

Our site uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

**Google Analytics** - Web analytics service. [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

**Google AdSense** - Advertising service. [Google's Ad Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

**Beehiiv** - Email newsletter platform. [Beehiiv's Privacy Policy](https://www.beehiiv.com/privacy)

## Your Rights

You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link at the bottom of any email. You may use browser settings to block cookies. You may contact us to request deletion of any personal data we hold.

## Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at individuals under the age of 21. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
