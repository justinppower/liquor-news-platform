We want to hear from you. Whether you have a news tip, press release, advertising inquiry, or feedback, here is how to reach the LiquorNewsUSA team.

## Editorial

Have a story tip, press release, or correction? Send it to our editorial desk.

**Email:** [editorial@liquornewsusa.com](mailto:editorial@liquornewsusa.com)

We review every submission. Priority goes to breaking industry news, Texas market stories, regulatory changes, and exclusive data. Include relevant links, contacts, and images where possible.

## Advertising & Sponsorships

Interested in display ads, sponsored content, newsletter sponsorship, or the Texas Spotlight? See our [advertising page](/advertise/) for placements and rates.

**Email:** [ads@liquornewsusa.com](mailto:ads@liquornewsusa.com)

## Newsletter

Questions about your newsletter subscription, delivery issues, or content preferences? Contact our newsletter team.

**Email:** [newsletter@liquornewsusa.com](mailto:newsletter@liquornewsusa.com)

Or manage your subscription directly from the link at the bottom of any newsletter email.

## General Inquiries

For anything else, including partnership proposals, speaking requests, or media inquiries:

**Email:** [contact@liquornewsusa.com](mailto:contact@liquornewsusa.com)

We aim to respond within 2 business days.
