For sellers

Selling your liquor store

You built it over years. We help you sell it for the right price, to the right buyer, without your staff or customers hearing about it first.

1

Readiness audit

We go through three years of numbers, your lease, permit history, stock and equipment. You get a written report with a likely price range and a list of fixes ranked by what they add. What the audit covers

2

Get ready to sell

Cleaner books, a longer lease, dead stock cleared, records pulled together. We help you do the work that moves the price most.

3

List and find the buyer

We set the price, prepare the sale package and take it to our buyers. Every buyer signs an NDA and shows proof of funds before seeing your name or numbers.

4

Close the sale

We negotiate the offer, run due diligence, coordinate the stock count and keep the buyer's TABC permit moving so your closing date holds.

What to have ready

  • Tax returns, last 3 years
  • Profit and loss statements
  • Texas sales tax reports
  • Your lease and renewal options
  • TABC permit details
  • Inventory list at cost
  • Equipment and coolers list
  • Deed, if you own the property

Missing some of these is normal. Pulling them together is part of step 2.

Own the building too?

Sell the store and real estate together, sell the store and lease the building to the buyer, or sell them separately. Jeff values the property and handles the real estate side, so you work with one team from start to finish.
